Public Stunt is a bold, expansive sans-serif typeface that demands attention with its heavy, clean-cut lines and powerful presence. Inspired by industrial machinery and modern utility, it conveys a sense of stability and strength, with an unmistakable wide, fat build that exudes authority. Each character is meticulously crafted to offer clear readability, making it ideal for bold headlines and statements that require a robust visual impact.

This font’s oversized, uniform structure and clean design give it a contemporary, almost mechanical feel, evoking the efficiency and precision of industrial tools and equipment. Perfect for settings where clarity and strength must be front and center, Public Stunt feels right at home in contexts that call for boldness and authority.

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures, and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability.  
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from Northern Europe to Southern Africa, from America to Southeast Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers. 

Designed by Mans Greback, Public Stunt combines strength and clarity in every letter, standing out as a compelling choice for impactful design.
